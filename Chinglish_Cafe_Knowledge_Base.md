# Chinglish.Cafe Project Context & Knowledge Base

## 1. Project Overview
*   **Site Name:** Chinglish.Cafe
*   **Target Audience:** International English speakers (foreigners), linguists, and culture enthusiasts.
*   **Tone of Voice:** Humorous, cultural, slightly academic but highly engaging.
*   **Core Goal:** To archive literal translations, internet slang, and historical pidgin phrases that showcase the humor and creativity of the Chinese language meeting the English world.

## 2. Content Structure (Hugo Front Matter Schema)
Whenever generating a new entry, strictly follow this YAML structure:

```yaml
---
title: "English Title Here"
date: YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS+08:00
category: "Daily Life" | "Workplace" | "Internet Slang" | "Other"
tags: ["Chinglish", "tag1", "tag2"]
chinglish_phrase: "The exact Chinglish phrase"
chinese_meaning: "中文词义 (pinyin)"
native_expression: "The natural/native English equivalent"
is_dictionary_added: true | false
humor_index: 1 to 5 (Integer)
example_sentence: "A practical English example sentence."
---
```

## 3. Standard Markdown Body Structure
1.  **## 📖 Origin Story**: A pure English explanation of the historical or cultural background.
2.  **## 🤓 Analysis & Usage**: A pure English analysis of its grammar (or lack thereof) and how it's used today.
