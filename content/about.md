Welcome to **Chinglish.Cafe**! ☕️

We are not just a dictionary; we are a cultural archive. Here, we document **English expressions born from Chinese logic**.

For a long time, Chinglish has been dismissed as just "bad English" or translation errors. But we believe it's something much more fascinating: a vibrant linguistic bridge, and a new, highly creative dialect forming in real-time. 

From hilarious literal translations on public signs to the wildest Chinese internet slang, we are building the ultimate dictionary of these **linguistic gems where cultures collide**. We celebrate the creativity, the confusion, and the comedy of language.
