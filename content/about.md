Welcome to **Chinglish.Cafe**! ☕️

Chinglish is often misunderstood as just "bad English." But here, we believe it's something much more fascinating: a vibrant linguistic bridge, a testament to the collision of Eastern and Western cultures, and, quite frankly, a fantastic source of humor.

Our mission is to archive this unique cultural phenomenon. From hilarious literal translations on public signs to the wildest Chinese internet slang (Internet buzzwords), we are building the ultimate dictionary of Chinese-English expressions.

We celebrate the creativity, the confusion, and the comedy of language.
