## 📖 Origin Story
"怎么是你？怎么老是你？" (zěn me shì nǐ? zěn me lǎo shì nǐ?) is a classic Chinese homophonic pun that has been circulating in classrooms and internet forums for decades. It plays on the uncanny phonetic resemblance between two of the most basic English greeting questions and a pair of increasingly exasperated Chinese accusations.

The Chinglish magic works like this:

* **"How are you":** When spoken with a playful Chinese accent, it sounds almost exactly like "怎么是你" (zěn me shì nǐ) — "Why is it you?" or "How did it end up being you?"
* **"How old are you":** Add one word, and it morphs into "怎么老是你" (zěn me lǎo shì nǐ) — "Why is it always you?" The insertion of "old" (lǎo / 老) transforms surprise into weary annoyance.

## 😂 Why It's Hilarious
This is arguably the most iconic Chinglish pun ever invented. It takes the very first English sentences most Chinese students learn in school and weaponizes them into a perfect comedic one-two punch.

The humor lies in the sheer absurdity of a polite Western greeting being reinterpreted as an exasperated Chinese complaint. "How are you?" — a phrase drilled into students as the gold standard of English politeness — suddenly becomes an accusation of unwanted coincidence. And "How old are you?" — a standard question about age — becomes the cry of someone haunted by a recurring nuisance.

It is the linguistic equivalent of discovering that your kindergarten alphabet song secretly contains a roast. Generations of Chinese students have giggled in the back of English class, unable to unhear the hidden Chinese meaning every time a foreigner politely asks about their well-being.

## ✍️ Usage in Context
Modern netizens use this phrase whenever they bump into the same person repeatedly in awkward or unwanted situations — exes, annoying colleagues, overzealous salespeople, or that one friend who always shows up uninvited.

**Example Conversation:**
* **A:** *(Walking into a coffee shop)* "Oh no."
* **B:** *(Already sitting there, waving enthusiastically)* "Hey! Fancy seeing you here!"
* **A:** *(Sighing)* "How are you? How old are you? I came to this neighborhood specifically to avoid you."
* **B:** "I'm fine, thanks! I'm 26. Why do you ask?"
* **A:** "Never mind. I'm moving to another city."
