## 📖 Origin Story
The original Chinese phrase "放马过来" is an ancient battlefield taunt. It translates roughly to "Release your horses and charge at me!" It is a heroic and aggressive way to challenge an opponent, commonly seen in historical Chinese dramas and martial arts movies.

---

## 🤓 Analysis & Usage
When translated directly into English, all the fierce, warlike energy completely vanishes. "Let the horse come here" sounds like a polite request from a farmer trying to guide a confused animal into a barn. If you shout this during an argument with an English speaker, they won't feel threatened—they will likely just look around wondering where the horse is.
