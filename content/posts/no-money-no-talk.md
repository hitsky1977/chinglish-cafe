## 📖 Origin Story
Originating from Hong Kong and the early trading days in Southern China, this phrase is a perfectly efficient, grammatically stripped-down way to establish business boundaries. It perfectly encapsulates the pragmatic business culture of the region.

## 🌐 Cultural Impact
Unlike other Chinglish phrases that remain inside jokes, "No money no talk" has practically breached the mainstream. It is so brutally honest, catchy, and efficient that many native English speakers in Asia use it naturally in everyday conversation. It is a triumph of Chinglish minimalism.
