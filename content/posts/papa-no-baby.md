## 📖 Origin Story
This phrase belongs to the wild, untamed tier of "Street Chinglish." It is widely circulated as a legendary (and perhaps slightly mythologized) bad translation spotted on unregulated medical clinic billboards in China that advertise infertility treatments. 

## 😂 Why It’s Hilarious
Instead of using complex, intimidating medical jargon like "reproductive endocrinology," this translation goes straight for the emotional jugular. It sounds less like a medical condition and more like the tragic title of an indie folk song or a sorrowful lament. It represents the absolute peak of "machine translation gone wild."
