## 📖 Origin Story
This phrase is a direct, character-by-character translation of the traditional Chinese idiom "人山人海" (rén shān rén hǎi). In Chinese, using the imagery of mountains and oceans to describe a massive crowd is a common and poetic hyperbole. 

When translated literally into English, it completely ignores all rules of grammar, but the visual impact is surprisingly strong and perfectly conveys the feeling of being overwhelmed by a massive crowd.

---

## 🤓 Analysis & Usage
While "a sea of people" is a perfectly acceptable idiom in standard English, adding the "mountain" takes the exaggeration to a hilarious new level. It is arguably one of the most famous Chinglish phrases on the internet. It became so legendary that it was actually submitted to and accepted by the Urban Dictionary. It is the perfect phrase to describe tourist attractions during a public holiday in China!
