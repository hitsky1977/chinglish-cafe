## 📖 Origin Story
This is a legendary example of early machine translation (or a very bad pocket dictionary) failing spectacularly. In Chinese, "表" (biǎo) means "cousin" (specifically cousins outside of the paternal lineage). However, the exact same character "表" also means a "watch" (as in a wristwatch). "妹" (mèi) simply means younger sister. 

---

## 🤓 Analysis & Usage
When translation software encounters "表妹", it sometimes mistakenly splits the characters and chooses the most common noun definition for the first character, resulting in "Watch sister." This creates a bizarre, slightly dystopian, or superhero-sounding title. It perfectly illustrates why translating Chinese characters individually without understanding the compound context is a recipe for comedic disaster.
