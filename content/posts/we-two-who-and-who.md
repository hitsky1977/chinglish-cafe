## 📖 Origin Story
When a Chinese person wants to emphasize an unbreakable bond or intimate friendship, they use the rhetorical question "我俩谁跟谁？" (Literally: "Between us two, who is with who?"). It implies that the boundary between the two individuals is so blurred by friendship that there is no need for extreme politeness or keeping score.

## 😂 Why It’s Hilarious
Translated word-for-word, it sounds like two people suffering from severe amnesia trying to figure out their own identities. Yet, within the context of Chinglish, it has become a deeply heartwarming declaration of "bromance" or best-friendship.
