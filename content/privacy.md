Welcome to **Chinglish.Cafe**. Your privacy is critically important to us.

### 1. Information We Collect
Currently, Chinglish.Cafe operates as a static website. We do not require you to create an account, nor do we collect sensitive personal data like passwords or credit card information. 

If you contact us via email to submit a phrase, we will only use your email address to reply to your submission. We will not sell, rent, or share your email with third parties.

### 2. Cookies and Analytics
Like almost all professional websites, this site may use cookies (tiny files downloaded to your computer) to improve your experience. 

We may use third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to understand how visitors engage with our site (e.g., which pages are the most popular, and where our traffic comes from). These services may collect non-personally identifying information like your browser type, language preference, and the date/time of your visit.

### 3. External Links
Our dictionary entries may contain links to external sites (such as Wikipedia or social media) for further reading. Please note that we have no control over the content and practices of these sites, and cannot accept responsibility or liability for their respective privacy policies.

### 4. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We encourage visitors to frequently check this page for any changes.
