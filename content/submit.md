Did you spot a ridiculously funny sign at a restaurant? Or maybe you just learned a new piece of Chinese internet slang that blew your mind? 

**We want it!** 📖

Chinglish.Cafe is a community-driven archive, and we rely on eagle-eyed contributors like you to keep this dictionary growing. 

### What makes a great submission?

When submitting a phrase, please try to include the following details so we can create a high-quality entry:

1. **The Chinglish Phrase:** The exact text you saw or heard (e.g., "Open water").
2. **The Original Chinese:** If you know it (e.g., "开水").
3. **The Native Meaning:** What it *actually* means in standard English (e.g., "Boiled water").
4. **Context / Photo:** Where did you see it? (If you have a photo of the sign or menu, PLEASE attach it—it makes the entry 100x better!).

### How to Submit

Currently, the fastest way to get your phrase published is to send us an email directly:

📩 **Send your submissions to:** [submit@chinglish.cafe](mailto:submit@chinglish.cafe)

*Don't forget to let us know what name or nickname you'd like us to use to credit you for the discovery!* We review all submissions weekly and will notify you if your phrase makes it into the Oxford/Urban Dictionary hall of fame.
