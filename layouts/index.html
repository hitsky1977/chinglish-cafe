💡 Did You Know?

📖

"Long time no see" first appeared in English literature in 1900.

It was famously used in W.F. Drannan's book Thirty-One Years on the Plains and in the Mountains, marking early Chinglish entering mainstream English.

🏆

"Add oil" (加油) was officially added to the Oxford English Dictionary in 2018.

Recognized as a legitimate expression of encouragement, proving that Chinglish is not just "broken English", but a new dialect forming in real-time.
